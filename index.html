      Add a stock pick

      Enter the price when picked, or leave it blank to use the closing price on that date (the previous trading day for weekends and holidays). Signed-in editors save picks to the shared club portfolio.

      Manage club portfolio

      Enter total cost and a fallback total value in dollars. For automatic stock valuations, also enter the ticker and number of shares. Existing holdings need these two fields added once. Cost is deducted from the $10,000 fund; the remainder stays in cash.

        Record a portfolio valuation

        Save today's total or enter a past portfolio value. Recording the same date replaces that day's value. The starting date is not set.

          Keep a copy of your stock data

          Download all stock picks, their latest loaded prices, club holdings, classifications, and recorded valuations as a text file. Save a new copy after making changes. This is a readable backup, not an encrypted file.
